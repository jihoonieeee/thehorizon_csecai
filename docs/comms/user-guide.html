Page 1
Overview

The main intelligence dashboard. Shows a synthesised view of the AI threat landscape for the selected time window — Last Week, Last Month, or Last Quarter. Use the segmented control at the top right to switch. The refresh button (↺) reloads data on demand; the page also auto-refreshes every 5 minutes.

Summary stat row

At the top, a row of counts shows the total sources in the window broken down by each threat category, plus an "Other" count for adjacent/defensive sources outside the four offensive categories.

Category cards

Four cards — one per threat category — each showing a source count, sparkline trend, evidence maturity bar, a one-sentence posture assessment, and a numbered list of insights. Click any insight to expand it for the full body, supporting bullet points, and clickable source citations.

The four categories are:

  • Traditional AI Threats — attacks on ML models: adversarial examples, data poisoning, model extraction, backdoors
  • LLM Threats — prompt injection, jailbreaks, RAG poisoning, guardrail bypass, data leakage
  • Agentic AI Threats — autonomous agent abuse, MCP vulnerabilities, tool misuse, coding agent risks
  • AI-Enabled Attacks — AI used as an attack tool: deepfakes, AI-generated phishing, voice cloning

Evidence maturity bar

Each category card includes a segmented bar showing how mature the evidence is for threats in that category. Click any segment to open a side panel listing the sources at that maturity level.

LevelWhat it means
ResearchDemonstrated in papers or controlled lab environments only — no adversary use
DemonstratedWorking exploit or PoC exists and is reproducible against a real product
DisclosedConfirmed vulnerability in a specific product — no working public exploit yet
ObservedConfirmed in real-world use against real victims — at least one documented incident
OperationalIn sustained or scaled use by one or more threat actors across multiple incidents

Trend chart

A multi-line weekly source volume chart appears below the category cards, showing ingestion volume per category over time. Useful for spotting emerging spikes in a particular threat area.

Top threats

A ranked list of the highest-importance sources in the period, with maturity badge, publisher, date, and category. Click any title to open the original article.

Taxonomy coverage

A grouped tag matrix showing how many sources are tagged with each specific technique, organised by category. Click any tag to open a drilldown panel listing the sources for that technique.

Page 2
Ask Agent

A chat interface for querying the ingested corpus in natural language. The agent searches the corpus, retrieves relevant evidence, and streams a structured answer with inline source citations. It searches the last 90 days by default — specify a timeframe in your question for broader or narrower results.

Getting started

On first load, six suggested prompts appear as clickable cards. Click one to send it immediately, or type your own question in the input bar and press Enter or the send button (↑).

Writing effective questions

Narrow your question by technique, category, actor, or timeframe. Broad questions produce generic answers.

"What prompt injection techniques have been demonstrated against production LLMs in the last three months?"
"Are there documented cases of autonomous AI agents being exploited in the wild?"
"What should defenders watch in the next 90 days?"
"Tell me about MCP vulnerabilities in the past 90 days"

Reading the response

Responses are structured with numbered findings, bold key terms, and inline citation numbers (e.g. [src-1]). Below each answer, clickable source buttons link to the original articles. A confidence level and temporal scope are shown below the answer.

🖼

If the agent answers from general background knowledge rather than the corpus (e.g. if no relevant sources were found), a "General knowledge — not grounded in the corpus" badge appears above the response. Treat these answers with additional caution.

Your conversation history is saved in your browser and persists across sessions. Use Clear history at the bottom of the input bar to start fresh.

The agent does not search the open web. All answers are drawn from ingested and processed sources. Very recent articles (last 24–48 hours) may not yet be in the corpus.
Page 3
Sources

A full table of every ingested and validated source. Use this page to browse the corpus, verify specific articles, or investigate a particular threat area in depth.

🖼

Time period

Use the pill switcher in the top-right to filter by 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or All time.

Category tabs

Tabs across the top filter sources by threat category. The count on each tab reflects the current active filters. Selecting a category tab reveals sub-tag filter pills — click one or more to narrow by specific technique.

Filters

  • Label — filter by reading value: Essential, Recommended, Analyst, Background
  • Importance — filter by evidence maturity: Operational, Observed, Disclosed, Demonstrated, Research
  • ★ Starred — show only sources you have starred
  • 🚩 Flagged — show sources marked for review
  • 📄 Reports — show landscape reports and their extracted sub-findings

Sorting

Sort by Importance (evidence maturity, highest first), Newest (publish date), or Ingested (when the source entered the system).

Source rows

Each row shows the title (linked to the original article), importance badge, reading value label, publisher, category, up to 3 taxonomy tags, and publish date. Click the ★ to star a source or ⚐ to flag it for review. Click anywhere on the row to expand the detail panel.

Detail panel

The expanded panel shows the full summary, evidence maturity and reading value, source type, trust tier, taxonomy tags, and the classification rationale. For landscape reports, it also shows extracted attack walkthroughs, critical insights, and observed trends. For MITRE ATLAS case studies, it shows the full attack chain with technique IDs and an optional flow diagram.

The Legend and Taxonomy buttons in the top-right open reference panels explaining all labels, maturity levels, and taxonomy tag codes.
Page 4
Generate

Access pre-generated reports. Two tabs: Slides and Newsletter. Reports are generated automatically on a schedule — there is no on-demand generation trigger in the dashboard.

🖼
Screenshot: Generate Page — Slides Tab

Slides

Select a reporting period — 1 Month, 1 Quarter, or 1 Year — to load the latest pre-generated deck for that window. The panel shows the coverage dates, source count, and slide count. Click ↓ Download PPTX to download the file.

Each deck includes: executive summary, per-category threat developments and analysis, 3–5 key insights per category, case study slides with attack-chain diagrams, and source citations on every slide. Edit the deck before presenting externally.

Newsletter

Select Past week or Past month to load the latest newsletter for that window. A live preview renders in the panel. Click Copy for email to copy the formatted HTML to your clipboard, then paste directly into Gmail or Outlook compose.
