Dashboard Overview
Navigation

After logging in, the dashboard opens to the Overview page. The navigation bar at the top provides access to four pages.

PageWhat it's for
OverviewAI threat landscape — per-category insights, evidence maturity, trend chart, top threats, and taxonomy coverage
Ask AgentNatural-language Q&A against the ingested corpus with streamed, cited answers
SourcesFull source table — browse, filter by category and technique, and drill into individual articles
GenerateDownload pre-generated briefing slide decks (PPTX) and copy the latest newsletter for email
Page 1
Overview

The main intelligence view. Shows the AI threat landscape for the selected time window — Last Week, Last Month, or Last Quarter. Use the segmented control at the top right to switch. The ↺ button reloads data; the page also auto-refreshes every 5 minutes.

Category cards

One card per threat category. Click any insight to expand it for the full body, bullet points, and source citations.

The four categories are:

  • Traditional AI Threats — attacks on ML models, e.g. adversarial examples, data poisoning, model extraction, backdoors
  • LLM Threats — e.g. prompt injection, jailbreaks, RAG poisoning, guardrail bypass, data leakage
  • Agentic AI Threats — autonomous agent abuse, e.g. MCP vulnerabilities, tool misuse, coding agent risks
  • AI-Enabled Attacks — AI as an attack tool, e.g. deepfakes, AI-generated phishing, voice cloning

Evidence maturity bar

A coloured bar on each card breaks down sources by how well-evidenced they are — from lab research through to threats confirmed in the wild. Click any segment to see the sources behind it.

LevelWhat it means
ResearchDemonstrated in papers or controlled lab environments only — no adversary use
DemonstratedWorking exploit or PoC exists and is reproducible against a real product
DisclosedConfirmed vulnerability in a specific product — no working public exploit yet
ObservedConfirmed in real-world use against real victims — at least one documented incident
OperationalIn sustained or scaled use by one or more threat actors across multiple incidents

Top threats

A ranked list of the highest-importance sources in the period, with maturity badge, publisher, date, and category. Click any title to open the original article.

Taxonomy coverage

A tag matrix showing how many sources are tagged with each specific technique, organised by category. Click any tag to open a panel listing sources for that technique. For a full explanation of all taxonomy codes and technique definitions, go to the Sources page and click the Taxonomy button in the top-right.

Page 2
Ask Agent

A chat interface for querying the ingested corpus in plain language. The agent searches the corpus, retrieves relevant evidence, and streams a structured answer with inline source citations. It searches the last 90 days by default — specify a timeframe in your question for broader or narrower results.

Getting started

On first load, six suggested prompts appear as clickable cards:

  • Most important finding
  • LLM jailbreak trends
  • Agentic AI risks
  • MCP vulnerabilities
  • AI as an attack tool
  • Defender watch list

Click one to send it immediately, or type your own question and press Enter or ↑.

Writing effective questions

Narrow your question by technique, category, actor, or timeframe. Broad questions produce generic answers.

"What prompt injection techniques have been demonstrated against production LLMs in the last three months?"
"Are there documented cases of autonomous AI agents being exploited in the wild?"
"What should defenders watch in the next 90 days?"
"Tell me about MCP vulnerabilities in the past 90 days"

Reading the response

Responses are structured with numbered findings, bold key terms, and inline citation numbers. Below each answer, source buttons link to the original articles. A confidence level and temporal scope are shown below the response.

If no relevant sources were found, a "General knowledge — not grounded in the corpus" badge appears above the response. Treat these answers with additional caution.

Conversation history is saved in your browser and persists across sessions. Use Clear history at the bottom of the input bar to start fresh.

Note
The agent does not search the open web — all answers are drawn from ingested and processed sources.

Very recent articles (last 24–48 hours) may not yet be in the corpus.

Dates reflect when a source was published, not necessarily when the underlying incident occurred — a report published this week may describe an incident that occurred earlier.
Page 3
Sources

A full table of every ingested and validated source. Use this page to browse the corpus, verify specific articles, or investigate a particular threat area in depth. The Legend and Taxonomy buttons in the top-right open reference panels explaining all labels, maturity levels, and taxonomy tag codes.

Time period

Use the pill switcher in the top-right to filter by 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or All time.

Category tabs

Tabs across the top filter sources by threat category. Selecting a category reveals sub-tag filter pills — click one or more to narrow by specific technique.

Filters

  • Label — filter by reading value: Essential, Recommended, Analyst, Background
  • Importance — filter by evidence maturity: Operational, Observed, Disclosed, Demonstrated, Research
  • ★ Starred — show only sources you have starred
  • Reports — show longer reports and their extracted sub-findings

Sorting

Sort by Importance (evidence maturity, highest first) or Newest (publish date).

Detail panel

Click any row to expand its detail panel, showing the full summary, evidence maturity and reading value, source type, trust tier, taxonomy tags, and classification rationale. For longer reports, it may also show extracted attack walkthroughs, critical insights, and observed trends. This feature is still being developed and content may vary.

Page 4
Generate

Access pre-generated reports. Two tabs: Slides and Newsletter. Reports are generated automatically on a schedule — there is no on-demand generation trigger in the dashboard.

Slides

Select a reporting period — 1 Month, 1 Quarter, or 1 Year — then click ↓ Download PPTX. Each deck includes an executive summary, per-category threat developments, and key insights with source citations.

Slides are AI-generated and must be reviewed and edited by a human before use. Verify claims, check citations, and adjust framing as appropriate before presenting or sharing externally.

Newsletter

Select Past week or Past month to load the latest newsletter. A live preview renders in the panel. Click Copy for email to copy the newsletter to your clipboard, then paste directly into Gmail or Outlook.

If a deck or newsletter shows "Not available yet", it has not been generated for that period. Check back after the next scheduled pipeline run.
