The Horizon
AI threat intelligence dashboard — prototype access

Dear colleagues,

AI is not only changing the way we work, it also brings new threats and risks. To make these developments more accessible, the AI team in CSEC invites you to explore The Horizon, our AI Threat Intelligence dashboard.

Explore the AI threat intelligence dashboard →

You do not need to be an AI specialist to use it. The dashboard is designed to help anyone quickly explore the evolving intersection of AI and cyber threats, discover emerging issues, and better understand why they may matter.

Whether you work in engineering, assurance, consultancy or tech policy functions, The Horizon offers a simple starting point for staying informed and asking the right questions.

Take a few minutes to explore the dashboard and make sense of what is appearing on the horizon. Your feedback will also help us make it more useful, accessible, and relevant to a wider range of users.

We hope you find it insightful in making sense of the AI threat landscape.

On behalf of the AI team,

Melvyn
